# Phase 5 — Issues and Material Condition

- Version: v2
- Status: Planned
- Supersedes: [v1](v1.md)
- Depends on: Phase 4
- Blocks: Phase 6

## Objective

Extend the repository-backed Issues surface into durable operational exception management. Issues can be recorded, assigned, discussed, resolved, dismissed, and reopened without losing history. Damaged material—including material that may need to be returned to a supplier—must retain photo evidence of the damage.

## Real-world workflow

1. Record an Issue manually or open one created by Receiving, verification, movement, or outbound.
2. Confirm its type, priority, site, linked project/lot/receipt/location, condition evidence, and blocking impact.
3. For a Damaged Issue, capture or upload at least one clear damage photo before the Issue can be recorded. A damage Issue created from Receiving may reuse the receipt line's required material photo.
4. Assign the Issue by name, add comments or additional optional photos, and record operational follow-up such as preparing damaged material for supplier return.
5. Resolve with a required resolution note, dismiss with a reason, or reopen when the problem returns.
6. Review all comments, evidence, and transitions in the Issue and global activity histories.

## Domain and public interfaces

- Preserve the current repository-backed `Issue`, existing types, durable operator names, readiness blocking, and automatic Receiving links.
- Expand Issue status to Open, In Progress, Resolved, and Dismissed while preserving reopening as an immutable transition back to active work.
- Preserve Low, Normal, High, and Urgent priorities unless the fresh implementation audit finds a user-approved reason to rename Urgent.
- Add linked-entity references for project, lot, receipt, location, movement, and outbound records.
- Add `IssueComment` and `IssueTransition` with actor name, optional future user ID, timestamp, and optional media.
- Keep condition status on the lot; Issues explain and track exceptions rather than replacing current condition.
- A Damaged Issue is invalid without at least one linked Photo Record that serves as damage evidence.

## Data and services

- Add narrow Issue list/detail, create, assign, comment, transition, evidence, and link-resolution use cases on the established Inventory transaction boundary.
- Validate required damage evidence in the domain service, not only in the form.
- Allow a Damaged Issue created during Receiving to link the receipt line's required material photo instead of requiring a duplicate upload.
- Route all automatic Issue creation through idempotent use cases so retrying an originating command does not duplicate Issues.
- Resolving an Unknown Shipment may link its Receipt to a Project but cannot rewrite the original identity evidence.
- Blocking Issues participate in Phase 4 readiness calculations in every active state and stop blocking only when Resolved or Dismissed.
- Preserve damage photos, comments, transitions, and links in versioned IndexedDB migrations and portable backups.

## UI requirements

- Evolve the current repository-backed `/inventory/issues` surface; do not restore the obsolete hard-coded implementation described by the original roadmap.
- Add filtering by state, type, priority, site, project, assignee, and age.
- Add Issue detail history and reusable Record Issue entry points from project, lot, location, receipt, movement, and outbound views.
- When Issue type is Damaged, show a required camera/upload control, clear evidence guidance, preview/removal controls, and accessible validation. Disable completion until at least one damage photo is linked.
- For other Issue types, photos remain optional unless the originating workflow has its own stricter evidence rule.
- Use text and icons rather than color alone for priority and state.

## Desktop behavior

- Use a dense Issue list with persistent filters and a detail panel or page for assignment, discussion, evidence, and resolution.
- Display damage evidence prominently without displacing linked record details or immutable history.
- Preserve drill-through to every linked operational record.

## Mobile behavior

- Emphasize Record Issue, camera-first damage capture, priority, handling risk, location, and quick assignment/follow-up.
- Use large controls, full-height sheets, sticky actions, and a chronological conversation/history view.
- A mobile operator cannot complete a Damaged Issue until a captured or selected photo has been retained successfully.

## Migration and rollback

- Migrate current foundation Issues into the expanded schema with their type, state, links, operator, and activity preserved.
- Existing Damaged Issues created before this rule remain readable and are marked as needing damage evidence; migration must not invent a photo or silently discard the Issue.
- Reuse valid Receiving material photos when an existing Damaged Issue and its receipt line are already linked.
- Completed transitions are append-only. Rollback leaves Issues readable and disables unsupported transitions.

## Out of scope

Supplier directories, return merchandise authorization, freight or pickup scheduling, vendor claims/disputes, procurement tasks, email/Teams/SMS notifications, SLA automation, insurance claims, and AI damage analysis.

## Test plan

- Cover every type, priority, and transition; automatic creation; duplicate prevention; assignment; comments; media; resolution/dismiss/reopen; and linked-entity navigation.
- Prove that manual and automatic Damaged Issues cannot be created without damage evidence.
- Prove that Receiving damage Issues can reuse the receipt line's required photo without creating duplicate image blobs.
- Prove that non-damage Issue photos remain optional.
- Test legacy Damaged Issue migration, missing-evidence indicators, photo retention across reload and backup round-trip, unknown receipt resolution, and readiness blocking/unblocking.
- Verify age calculations, filters, append-only history, desktop/mobile flows, keyboard operation, touch targets, and accessibility.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every operational exception is durable, traceable, and linked to the affected work.
- Every newly created Damaged Issue retains at least one linked damage photo.
- Receiving-linked damage evidence is reused without requiring duplicate capture.
- Automatic retries never create duplicate Issues or photo records.
- Resolution never erases the original condition, evidence, comments, or transitions.
- Project readiness responds correctly to blocking Issue state.
- The rule that only Receiving universally requires material photos remains intact; the narrower Damaged Issue evidence rule is the explicit exception.

## Final report requirements

Use the [shared final report](../README.md#shared-final-report), including repository-backed Issue preservation, transition coverage, damage-photo enforcement, Receiving evidence reuse, legacy migration behavior, duplicate prevention, readiness integration, responsive/accessibility evidence, and confirmation that Phase 6 was not started.

## Decision log

- v1: Three-role authentication remains deferred; assignee and actor values are names until Phase 6.
- v1: Issues complement rather than overload material condition status.
- v2: Every Damaged Issue requires at least one linked damage photo; other Issue types keep optional evidence unless their originating workflow requires it.
- v2: Receiving-created damage Issues reuse the receipt line's required material photo when available.
- v2: An Issue may track damaged material pending supplier return, but supplier, claim, authorization, and shipping management remain outside Phase 5.
