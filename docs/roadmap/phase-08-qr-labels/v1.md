# Phase 8 — QR Labels

- Version: v1
- Status: Gated — implementation is not authorized
- Depends on: Phase 7 plus confirmed new-warehouse hardware and workflow
- Blocks: Nothing; Phases 9 and 10 may proceed

The Phase 6 [temporary shared-environment plan](../phase-06-supabase-authentication/temporary-validation-plan-v1.md) does not satisfy or bypass this hardware gate. It establishes authentication and site authorization only; no scan token, label, QR route, or print surface may be added under Phase 8 v1.

## Objective

Define the entry gate and intended safety boundaries for a future QR and thermal-label workflow without replacing the current handwritten project-label process prematurely.

## Real-world workflow

Receiving staff handwrite the project name on a physical label and attach it to the received box, pallet, crate, appliance, or assembly. Phase 2 records the label text, whether it was applied, and optional photographic evidence. That remains the production workflow until this phase receives a v2 plan.

## Entry gate

Before creating v2 or implementation code, confirm:

- The new warehouse operating layout and receiving/label placement process.
- Thermal printer make/model, connection method, supported languages, print density, and device management.
- Label stock dimensions, adhesive, outdoor/moisture/abrasion durability, and replacement availability.
- Required scanning distance, supported phones and any keyboard-wedge scanners.
- Which entities receive labels, where labels are placed, who can print/reprint/retire them, and what happens when packaging is split or replaced.
- Field-tested sample output with real, non-sensitive representative content.

## Planned domain and public interfaces

- Plan for opaque `ScanIdentifier`, `LabelRecord`, print job, reprint/retirement history, and target links to material lots and storage locations.
- QR payloads contain only an opaque identifier or short application URL; never project, PO, employee, quantity, material, or location details.
- Reserve authenticated `/scan/[token]` routing for scan-to-open, move, and verify.
- Preserve identifiers through label reprints while recording every print, retirement, and replacement.

## UI requirements

- Mobile camera scanning and keyboard-wedge scanner input must converge on the same authenticated resolver.
- An offline scan may resolve only identifiers already present in the assigned-site cache and must clearly show cached state.
- Printing must use field-tested TBS templates based on the confirmed hardware/stock; no label dimensions are selected in v1.

## Desktop behavior

- A future v2 may authorize print queues, preview, reprint, retirement, and audit history for permitted users.
- v1 adds no print surface, placeholder action, or nonfunctional control.

## Mobile behavior

- A future v2 may authorize camera-first scan-to-open, move, and verify plus keyboard-wedge input.
- v1 leaves the current handwritten-label and non-QR mobile workflows unchanged.

## Data, migration, and rollback

- No database table, dependency, route, QR token, or placeholder print button is added under v1.
- v2 must define token generation/rotation, authorization, print formats, printer integration, offline resolution, and rollback before implementation.
- The handwritten label record remains historical evidence after QR adoption.

## Out of scope

All QR generation, scanning, thermal printing, and hardware integration until the entry gate is satisfied; barcode/OCR recognition; public unauthenticated scan results.

## Test plan

No implementation tests are authorized in v1. The v2 plan must require token security tests, authorization, scan reliability, label durability, replacement/reprint, offline cached resolution, supported-device coverage, and field acceptance at the new warehouse.

## Acceptance criteria

- v1 is complete when its gate is documented and no premature QR/printing surface exists.
- Implementation may begin only from an approved v2 containing the confirmed hardware and field workflow.

## Final report requirements

If the gate is evaluated, report every confirmed and unresolved gate item, attach field-test results, and either create v2 or explicitly keep the phase gated. Do not report Phase 8 as implemented under this version.

## Decision log

- v1: Phase remains gated until the end-of-year warehouse and thermal-label system are known.
- v1: No default 4×6 or Letter format is assumed.
- v1: Phase is non-blocking for Yard Map and Reporting.
